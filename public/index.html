How to play Senet

The 5,000-year-old Egyptian race game. Get all five of your golden pawns off the board first.

  1. Cast the sticks — four lots tumble; the light faces count 1–4, all dark counts 5.
  2. Another turn on a throw of 1, 4 or 5.
  3. Move one pawn forward along the winding track. Click a glowing pawn to move it.
  4. Swap! Landing on a rival pawn trades places. Your own pawns block the way.
  5. House of Beauty (26) — every pawn must stop here; it can't be jumped over.
  6. House of Water (27) — splash! Back to the House of Rebirth (15).
  7. Houses 28–30 are sacred — rivals can't bump you there.
  8. Land exactly on 30 to bear a pawn off. No overshooting, ever.

You win! 🎉

The gods smile on your journey through the Duat.
